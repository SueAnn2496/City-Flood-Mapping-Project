Project Overview

Understanding Urban Flood & Waterlogging Risk

This WebGIS platform integrates selected thematic GIS outputs into an interactive web-based mapping environment. Users can explore Klang, Shah Alam and Petaling Jaya and compare spatial characteristics using the available thematic layers.

📐

Slope Analysis

Slope information describes terrain characteristics and influences surface-water movement and accumulation.

🌳

Land Cover

Identifies built-up areas, vegetation, water bodies, and other classes providing spatial context for waterlogging.

📍

Study Area

The boundary layer defines the spatial extent of selected study areas for comparing different thematic maps.

🔎 How to Interpret the Maps

Compare the thematic layers together to understand locations where terrain and land-cover characteristics contribute to potential waterlogging.

1. Check the Boundary Identify the selected study area before comparing information.
2. Compare Slope Observe the spatial distribution of gentle and steep terrain.
3. Compare Land Cover Examine built-up and vegetated areas together with slope patterns.

⚡ During a Flood

  • Shut off electricity & gas: If water levels rise, immediately turn off main power switches and gas valves to prevent electrocution or fires.
  • Move to higher ground: Quickly evacuate to the second floor, roof, or designated temporary evacuation centres (PPS).
  • Avoid driving or walking through moving water: Flowing water only 30cm deep can sweep away adults or vehicles; never attempt to cross flooded roads.
  • Stay connected: Keep your mobile phone charged, carry a power bank, and keep an emergency Grab Bag ready.

🧽 Post-Flood Safety & Cleanup

  • Beware of electrical hazards & wildlife: Check for damaged power lines; watch out for snakes, insects, or sharp debris in standing water.
  • Drinking water & food safety: Do not drink untreated tap water; always boil drinking water. Discard flood-damaged or unsealed food items.
  • Wear protective gear: Wear boots, gloves, and masks during cleanup to prevent infections like leptospirosis (rat urine disease).
  • Inspect structural integrity: Ensure your home has no structural or foundation damage before moving back inside.

🛡️ Preparedness & Prevention

Proactive planning and everyday readiness can significantly reduce property damage and safety risks during disasters.

1. Prepare an Emergency Grab Bag Include waterproofed important documents, first-aid items, flashlight, whistle, non-perishable food, bottled water, and a power bank.
2. Clear Drainage Regularly Inspect and clear surrounding drains and downpipes to prevent blockages and backflow during heavy downpours.
3. Monitor Weather & Alerts Stay updated via MetMalaysia forecasts, the Public Infobanjir portal, and this WebGIS risk platform.
