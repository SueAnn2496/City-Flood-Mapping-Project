Understanding Urban Flood & Waterlogging Risk

This WebGIS platform integrates selected thematic GIS outputs into an interactive web-based mapping environment. Users can explore Klang, Shah Alam and Petaling Jaya and compare spatial characteristics using the available thematic layers.

⚡ During a Flood

  • Shut off electricity & gas: If water levels rise, turn off the main power switch and gas valves to prevent electrocution or fires.
  • Move to higher ground: Go to the second floor, the roof, or a temporary evacuation centre (PPS).
  • Avoid moving water: Never try to drive or walk across flooded roads. Flowing water only 30 cm deep can sweep away adults or vehicles.
  • Stay connected: Keep your phone charged, carry a power bank, and keep an emergency grab bag ready.

🧽 Post-Flood Safety & Cleanup

  • Beware of electrical hazards & wildlife: Check for damaged power lines, and watch for snakes, insects and sharp debris in standing water.
  • Drinking water & food: Do not drink untreated tap water. Boil drinking water and discard flood-damaged or unsealed food.
  • Wear protective gear: Boots, gloves and masks help prevent infections such as leptospirosis during cleanup.
  • Check the building: Make sure the structure and foundation are safe before moving back in.

⚡ During a Flood

  • Shut off electricity & gas: Turn off main power switches to prevent electrocution.
  • Move to higher ground: Evacuate to the second floor or evacuation centres.
  • Avoid driving or walking through moving water: Never attempt to cross flooded roads.

🧽 Post-Flood Safety & Cleanup

  • Beware of electrical hazards & wildlife: Watch out for damaged power lines and debris.
  • Drinking water safety: Always boil drinking water; discard unsealed flood-damaged food.
  • Wear protective gear: Prevent infections like leptospirosis during cleanup.
