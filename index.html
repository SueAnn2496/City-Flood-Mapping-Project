Project Overview

Understanding Urban Flood & Waterlogging Risk

This WebGIS platform integrates selected thematic GIS outputs into an interactive web-based mapping environment. Users can explore Klang, Shah Alam and Petaling Jaya and compare their spatial characteristics using the available thematic layers.

📐

Slope Analysis

Slope information describes the terrain characteristics of the study area. Areas with different slope conditions can be compared to understand how terrain may influence surface-water movement and accumulation.

🌳

Land Cover

Land cover information identifies built-up areas, vegetation, water bodies and other land-cover classes. This provides important spatial context for interpreting urban waterlogging conditions.

📍

Study Area

The boundary layer defines the spatial extent of the selected study areas and provides the framework for comparing the different thematic maps.

🔎 How to Interpret the Maps

Flood susceptibility should not be interpreted from one factor alone. Users should compare the thematic layers together to understand locations where terrain and land-cover characteristics may contribute to potential waterlogging.

1. Check the Boundary Identify the selected study area before comparing the thematic information.
2. Compare Slope Observe the spatial distribution of gentle and steep terrain within the study area.
3. Compare Land Cover Examine built-up and vegetated areas together with the slope pattern.

Project Scope

  • Study areas: Klang, Shah Alam and Petaling Jaya.
  • Thematic maps currently available: Boundary Area, Slope and Land Cover.
  • Interactive functions include study-area selection, map-layer switching, legend display and satellite basemap control.
  • The thematic layers should be interpreted together rather than treating a single factor as a final flood-risk result.
Recommended enhancement: If historical flood/waterlogging locations or rainfall data are available, these datasets can be integrated as additional GIS evidence. A final flood-risk or susceptibility map should only be presented after a documented GIS analysis has been performed.
